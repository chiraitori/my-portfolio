# I have a girlfriend after 2 years! 💖

## How we met
It all started when I was teasing my friend about how jealous I was of him having a girlfriend. I told him I wanted one too. He suddenly asked, "Do you really want a girlfriend?" I said, "Yes, absolutely!"

He smiled and said, "Okay, let me help you." When he actually introduced me to someone, I was in complete shock. Like, *is this actually real?* At first, I just chatted with her like a normal friend.

## Dating
We started calling each other on Discord. Everything felt super natural and comfortable. After about 3 or 4 days of talking back and forth, I started feeling like we were really connecting, and hoped she might feel the same way soon.

Like any couple, we ended up texting each other all day long, and we'd play games together whenever we could. Finally, after 3 weeks, I gathered my courage, confessed my feelings to her, and she said yes! Confessing to her was the best decision I've made in a long time. 🥰

## Now
I am so, so happy right now because I've finally found my special someone. I promise to love and cherish her for a very long time. 

I hope you guys are happy for us, and I wish you all the best of luck in finding your own happiness too!
